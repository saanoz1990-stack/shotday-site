Support

The fastest way to reach the developer is inside the app: Settings › Contact us. Pick bug, idea or question, write a few words, and add your email if you'd like a reply. Your shots and health logs are not sent.

You can also email shotday.app1@gmail.com.

How do I add a widget?

Press and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Shotday. Choose what each widget shows in the app's Widgets tab. For the Lock Screen, press and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then the widget area.

I bought Shotday on another iPhone

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Moving to a new iPhone

In Settings, save a backup file, send it to your new iPhone (AirDrop, Files or email), then use Restore from a backup file. Shotday also saves one backup a day in the Files app (On My iPhone › Shotday › Backups).

Reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications for Shotday are on in the iPhone Settings app. If they are off, Shotday shows a button in its Settings to turn them on. Reminders start after you log your first real shot.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Shotday is on iPhone first, and an Android version is coming later.

Is the medication level accurate?

It's an estimate from published half-life data and your own shot times, shown as a range. Shotday is a log, not medical advice. Always follow your doctor and your pharmacy label.
